# `made`

How much did you make today? Harder to answer than you'd think. Put in your pre-tax salary and see what you made today, after taxes and with the benefits your company pays counted in.

<table>
<tr valign="top">
<td width="50%">
<h3>Drag your salary</h3>
<p>Slide the salary and today's number, the hourly rate, the year so far and what's left until December all recount as you go.</p>
</td>
<td width="50%">
<img src="demos/salary.gif" alt="Drag your salary">

</tr>

<h3>Set your taxes</h3>
<p>Pick single or married and your state. The tax rate defaults to the average for your income (federal, state and FICA), and you can drag it to your own.</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr valign="top">
<td width="50%">
<h3>Leave out the days you don't work</h3>
<p>Vacation, weekends, federal holidays and sick days come off the working year, with defaults from the averages for salaried jobs. Settings stay in your browser until you reset them.</p>
